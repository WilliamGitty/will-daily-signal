W12 & West London Property TfL to build more than 50 bus driver toilet blocks

More than 50 extra toilet blocks will be built for London's bus drivers in the next three years, Transport for London (TfL) has said. The organisation will fund an additional 53 blocks across the capital to ensure there were "suitable toilet facilities for drivers on every route". Six bus routes in London do not currently have toilet facilities at either terminus and 10 other routes have toilets at a terminus that may not be accessible throughout the whole route operating time, TfL said.
